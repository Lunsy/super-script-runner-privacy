Privacy Policy for Super Script Runner
Effective Date: August 11, 2025

Super Script Runner ("we", "our", or "the extension") does not collect, store, or share any personal information from its users.

What data do we collect?
We do not collect any personal data, browsing history, scripts, settings, or any other information from you. All your userscripts, settings, and related data are stored locally on your device using the browser's built-in chrome.storage API. We have no access to this data.

Third-party services
This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools. It does not communicate with any external servers except when you, the user, explicitly configure a userscript to fetch updates from a remote URL (e.g., Pastebin, GitHub). In those cases, the update check is performed directly from your browser to the remote URL; we do not intermediate or log these requests.
